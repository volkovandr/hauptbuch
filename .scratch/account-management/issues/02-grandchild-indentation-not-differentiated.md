# Account list only shows one level of indentation — grandchildren look like children

Status: needs-triage
Severity: minor
Area: Account management (account list screen, hierarchy rendering)

The accounts list distinguishes a parent from its direct children visually, but a grandchild
renders at the same indent level as a child — there's no visual difference between depth 1 and
depth 2+ in the hierarchy.

Request: indent each additional level further (or otherwise visually distinguish depth beyond one
level) so the tree structure is legible at a glance.
